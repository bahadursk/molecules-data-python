# How to Use This Book

## Reading paths

- **School and first-year students:** Part I, then chapters 13 (Matplotlib) and 20 (RDKit I). Look for 🟢 chapters.
- **Undergraduates:** Parts I–IV in order, dipping into Part V.
- **Researchers:** skim Part I, then go straight to Parts III, V and VI.
